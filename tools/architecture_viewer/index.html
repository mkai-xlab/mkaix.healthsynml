What happensGrade 1 F1 is 0.32 while G3 and G4 clear 0.73 and 0.81
Where the errors goAlmost entirely into the neighbouring grades 0 and 2, not far away — which is why QWK stays at 0.77 while accuracy is 0.60
Why QWK is the right metricKL is ordinal: confusing G0 with G1 is a small error, confusing G0 with G4 is not. Accuracy treats them the same, QWK does not.

Why keep two different backbones

DenseNet-121Dense concatenation reuses features; small (7 M) and the stronger of the two here
SE-ResNeXt-50Grouped convolutions plus channel attention; larger receptive capacity, different error pattern
The point of the pairTwo architectures that fail on different images average out better than either alone — the standard ensemble argument
